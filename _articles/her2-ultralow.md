the_issue: >
  The phase 3 DESTINY-Breast06 trial extended eligibility for trastuzumab deruxtecan to
  hormone-receptor-positive metastatic tumours with IHC 0 staining that nonetheless showed
  faint, incomplete membrane positivity -- a category subsequently termed HER2-ultralow.
  Because this pattern and true HER2-null status are both conventionally reported as IHC 0,
  the trial's eligibility criterion introduced a distinction that routine reporting was not
  previously required to make.
anchors:
  - citation: "Bardia A, et al. DESTINY-Breast06. N Engl J Med 2024;391:2110-2122"
    type: "RCT"
    note: >
      In the intention-to-treat population (n=866), trastuzumab deruxtecan was associated
      with a 36% relative reduction in the hazard of progression or death versus chemotherapy
      (HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.54-0.76). The HER2-ultralow subgroup (defined as IHC 0 with membrane
      staining) comprised 153 of 866 patients; a treatment benefit was also reported in this
      subgroup, though it was not the trial's primary comparison.
  - citation: "Wolff AC, et al. ASCO-CAP guideline update. J Clin Oncol 2023;41:3867-3872"
    type: "guideline"
    note: >
      Affirmed the 2018 ASCO-CAP recommendations without change. The panel explicitly declined
      to endorse a new IHC 0/1+ threshold or a "HER2-low" reporting category, citing insufficient
      evidence at the time of writing (published before DESTINY-Breast06 was reported).
  - citation: "Multicentre interobserver study of HER2-ultralow discrimination (36 raters, 50 IHC 0 cases)"
    type: "cohort study"
    note: >
      Reported Fleiss' kappa of 0.23 for the null-versus-ultralow distinction specifically,
      lower than kappa for the conventional four-tier 0/1+/2+/3+ classification (0.34) in the
      same cohort. High agreement (defined as concordance among at least 4 of 36 raters) was
      reached in only 4% of ultralow calls, versus 32% when null and ultralow were pooled as
      a single IHC 0 category.
solvable_now:
  - "The ultralow pattern is operationally defined as faint, incomplete membrane staining in ≤10% of tumour cells, within an otherwise IHC 0 case; this threshold derives from trial eligibility criteria rather than from an independently validated biological cut-point."
  - "Where treatment eligibility depends on the distinction, review at high power (40x) across the full tumour area, with concurrent positive and negative controls, is consistent with published best-practice guidance for the 0/1+ boundary generally."
  - "Independent second review is a reasonable mitigation where interobserver agreement is known to be low, though no controlled study has yet quantified how much second review improves concordance specifically for this distinction."
  - "Reporting the morphological pattern in descriptive terms, rather than assigning an unendorsed category label, avoids implying a degree of diagnostic certainty the evidence does not yet support."
not_solved:
  - "No published study has established a reproducible, validated threshold distinguishing HER2-null from HER2-ultralow; the current criterion is a trial-eligibility convention, not a biologically derived cut-point."
  - "Whether immunohistochemistry has adequate analytic sensitivity to detect the lowest levels of HER2 protein expression at all remains an open question in the cited literature."
  - "As of this writing, ASCO-CAP has not issued updated guidance incorporating DESTINY-Breast06; readers should check for more recent guideline updates before treating this article's framing as current."
sample_wording: >
  HER2 immunohistochemistry: score 0. Faint, incomplete membrane staining is present in
  approximately [x]% of tumour cells, below the threshold for score 1+.
personal_perspective: >
  In this author's practice, systematic review for ultralow-pattern staining is reserved for
  hormone-receptor-positive metastatic cases in which trastuzumab deruxtecan eligibility is
  clinically pending, rather than applied uniformly to all IHC 0 cases; the yield and clinical
  utility of universal screening for this pattern have not, to this author's knowledge, been
  formally assessed. Given the documented interobserver variability, an equivocal or borderline
  finding is communicated to the requesting clinician as such, rather than resolved unilaterally.
bottom_line: >
  HER2-ultralow is a trial-derived eligibility criterion, not a validated diagnostic category,
  and published interobserver agreement for the distinction is low (kappa 0.23, one study).
  Where eligibility may hinge on it, apply high-power review with controls, consider second
  review, and report the finding descriptively rather than as a definitive category.
